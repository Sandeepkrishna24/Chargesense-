## 1️⃣ Can a **car charge at a 2-wheeler charging station?**

### ❌ **NO (in almost all real-world cases)**

**Why:**

* **Electric cars** (Tata Nexon EV, Tigor EV, Tiago EV, Punch EV, etc. in your Excel) use:

  * **Type-2 (AC)** for slow/normal charging
  * **CCS2 (DC)** for fast charging
* **2-wheeler charging stations** (like older **Ather Grid**, Bounce, Hero, etc.) usually:

  * Use **low-power proprietary connectors**
  * Are designed for **small batteries & low current**

📌 Even if the plug *looks compatible*, the **power, protocol, and authentication** are different.

✅ **Exception (rare):**

* If a station is **explicitly marked as multi-vehicle / LECCS / CCS2 / Type-2 public charger**, then cars can charge.
* Most **pure 2-wheeler stations ≠ car-compatible**

**Conclusion:**
👉 A **car cannot charge at a dedicated 2-wheeler station**.
